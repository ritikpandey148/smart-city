HELP & SUPPORT

Guidelines

Please follow these guidelines while reporting an issue to help us maintain a clean, safe and better city.

Why Guidelines?

These guidelines ensure that your reports are genuine, accurate and helpful. They help the civic authorities to take faster and more effective action for a cleaner and safer city.

Your Responsible Reporting Makes a Real Difference

Report genuine issues, provide correct information and help build a better city for all.

Do's

What you should do while reporting

Upload a clear and real photo

Take a recent photo of the actual issue. Ensure the image is clear and taken from the location of the problem.

Provide correct location

Use the map or current location to select the exact spot. Also enter the correct area/landmark and pincode.

Give a meaningful title and description

Clearly explain the issue with proper details so that authorities can understand and take action quickly.

Select the correct issue type

Choose the right category (e.g. Overflowing Dustbin, Waste on Road, Illegal Dumping, Pothole, etc.) for your report.

Track your complaint

Check the status in 'My Complaints' section to stay updated on the action taken.

Don'ts

What you should avoid while reporting

Do not upload fake or AI generated images

Upload only real photos. Edited, fake or AI generated images are not allowed and may lead to rejection of your report.

Do not provide wrong or fake location

Do not intentionally give incorrect location. Fake locations may mislead authorities and such reports can be removed.

Do not submit false or duplicate reports

Do not report issues that do not exist or are already reported. Repeated false reports may result in restricted access.

Do not misuse the platform

Do not upload unrelated content, inappropriate images or use abusive language in title/description.

Do not report resolved or non-existent issues

Check if the issue is still present before reporting. Do not submit reports for already resolved or old issues.

Important Note

Anyone found submitting fake, misleading or inappropriate reports may face removal of the report and restricted access to the platform.

Responsible Reporting Builds a Better City

Let's work together to keep our city clean, safe and sustainable for a brighter tomorrow.
