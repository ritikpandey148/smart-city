ABOUT US

Building a Cleaner,
Safer and Smarter City

Smart City is a citizen-powered platform to report civic issues and contribute towards a cleaner, safer and better city for everyone.

Our Platform

Smart City is a digital platform that allows citizens to report civic issues like garbage, potholes and other infrastructure problems. The platform connects citizens with the authorities to ensure faster resolution and a cleaner, safer and more liveable city.

Our Objective

To empower citizens to actively participate in improving their city by making issue reporting simple, transparent and effective.

Our Vision

A cleaner, safer, smarter and more sustainable city where every citizen's voice matters.

Why Smart City?

We believe that small actions by citizens can create a big impact in building a better city.
