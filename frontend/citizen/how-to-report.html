HELP & SUPPORT

How to Report

Follow these simple steps to report a garbage issue and help keep our city clean, healthy and safe for everyone.

What is a Garbage Issue?

A garbage issue includes overflowing dustbins, uncollected waste, scattered garbage on roads, illegal dumping or any other waste-related problem in public places.

Your report helps us
  • Keep our city clean and healthy
  • Reduce pollution and disease
  • Make public places safer and better

Tips for a Better Report

Follow these tips to help us resolve your complaint faster.

Upload a clear and recent photo

Write a short and meaningful title

Select the exact location on map

Provide correct and detailed description
